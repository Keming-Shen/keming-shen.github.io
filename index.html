Keming Shen / 沈可铭

Keming Shen 沈可铭

I am a final-year M.Sc. student at the Computer Vision Institute (CVI), Shenzhen University (SZU), advised by Prof. Linlin Shen. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering (Tencent Cloud Artificial Intelligence program) from College of Computer Science and Software Engineering (CSSE), SZU in 2024.

我是深圳大学（SZU）计算机视觉研究所（CVI）的硕士三年级研究生，导师为沈琳琳教授。 在此之前，我于2024年获得深圳大学计算机与软件学院（CSSE）软件工程（腾讯云人工智能特色班）专业的工学学士学位。

My research interests include Computer Vision, 3D Human Motion Generation, Embodied AI, and Reinforcement Learning. I have submitted papers to CVPR, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICCV, and AAAI, with two papers accepted for publication at AAAI 2026 and CVPR 2025. I also served as a reviewer for ICCV 2025 and AAAI 2026. I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in Fall 2027 and research internships in human modeling, embodied AI, or AIGC, and research collaborations are welcome. For inquiries, please contact me by or via .

我的研究兴趣包括计算机视觉、三维人体动作生成、具身智能与强化学习。我曾在CVPR、NeurIPS、 ICLR、ICCV、AAAI等会议上投稿论文，并且已有两篇论文被AAAI 2026和CVPR 2025会议接受发表。此外，我还担任过ICCV 2025与AAAI 2026 计算机视觉方向的审稿人。 目前，我正在申请2027 年秋季的博士学位，同时寻找人体建模、具身智能算法、以及AIGC方向的研究实习，也欢迎相关领域的学者参与 研究合作。欢迎通过或者与我交流。

I excel at drawing, playing the electronic piano, and table tennis. I'm also keen on swimming, photography, cooking, and reading. I received the Bronze Prize in the 2016 "Tianyan" International Drawing Competition and hold a Level 10 certificate in electronic piano. I identify most closely with the ENFJ (Protagonist Personality) type. I have a knack for staying hopeful even in tough times, always reminding myself of what I have to be grateful for. I strive to be genuine, caring, and someone who walks the walk. Truly, nothing fulfills me more than inspiring others to do what’s right.

研究之外，我擅长绘画、电子琴和乒乓球，也喜欢游泳、摄影、烹饪与阅读。 我曾获 2016 年“天眼杯”国际绘画大赛铜奖，并持有电子琴十级证书。 我的性格类型最接近 ENFJ（主人公型人格）。于我而言，天生怀有正义感，真诚关爱他人， 言出必行，表里如一。在困境面前，始终能坚守希望，时刻铭记生活中总有诸多值得感恩之处。 激励他人践行正道，便是莫大的快乐。
