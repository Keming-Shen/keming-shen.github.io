Keming Shen 沈可铭

I am a final-year M.Sc. student at the Computer Vision Institute (CVI), Shenzhen University (SZU), advised by Prof. Linlin Shen. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering (Tencent Cloud Artificial Intelligence program) from SZU's College of Computer Science and Software Engineering (CSSE) in 2024.

My research interests include Computer Vision, 3D Human Motion Generation, Embodied AI, and Reinforcement Learning. I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in Fall 2027 and research internships in human modeling or AI-generated content (AIGC), and welcome research collaborations in these areas. For inquiries, please contact me by email at 2400101005@mails.szu.edu.cn or via WeChat at skm1192405754.

Outside research, I enjoy drawing, playing the electronic piano, table tennis, swimming, basketball, photography, cooking, and reading. I received the Bronze Prize in the 2016 Tianyan International Drawing Competition and hold a Level 10 certificate in electronic piano. I identify most closely with the ENFJ personality type.

I share technical notes and reflections on everyday life on Keming's Eutopia / Blog.
