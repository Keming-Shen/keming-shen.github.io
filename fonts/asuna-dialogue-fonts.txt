Asuna companion dialogue fonts (locally hosted WOFF2 subsets)

asuna-dialogue-zh.woff2
  Based on Xiaolai Regular v3.126, https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font
  Source: https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font/releases/download/v3.126/Xiaolai-Regular.ttf
  License: OFL-Xiaolai.txt
  Subset family: Asuna Dialogue Chinese

asuna-dialogue-en.woff2
  Based on Patrick Hand, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/patrickhand
  License: OFL-PatrickHand.txt
  Subset family: Asuna Dialogue English
  Used as the fallback when Comic Sans MS is unavailable locally.
  Comic Sans MS is referenced via CSS only, never redistributed.

Rebuild: python tools/asuna-rig/subset-dialogue-fonts.py
All dialogue characters and basic Latin are included. Rebuild after adding lines.
Copyright and license metadata are retained inside the font subsets.
