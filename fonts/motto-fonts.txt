Academic homepage motto fonts

These locally hosted WOFF2 subsets contain only the characters needed by the
homepage motto. Full upstream font files remain outside the published source.

motto-en.woff2
  Source: Caveat, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat
  Static weight: 500. License: OFL-Caveat.txt
  Subset family name: Academic Motto English

motto-zh.woff2
  Source: LXGW WenKai, https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai
  Weight: Regular. License: OFL-LXGWWenKai.txt
  Subset family name: Academic Motto Chinese
  Includes all characters and punctuation in the Chinese motto and attribution.

Copyright and license metadata are retained inside each subset. Regenerate the
corresponding subset if the motto text changes to include new characters.
